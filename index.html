Leadership & Vision

President's Message

It is with great pleasure that we welcome you to IGSM, International Graduate School of Ministry. IGSM is committed to excellence in leadership training and education for the evangelical churches both at home and abroad. Its primary role in the field of training and education is to offer a quality spiritual and academic program in the context of commitment to ministerial and spiritual values.

To this end, its curriculum is geared to the development of the faithful and competent church leader in each of its students. And we believe that such a curriculum will give each student the necessary foundation on which to build Christ’s church for His kingdom here in the Philippines and abroad.

We are glad and grateful for the opportunities offered here at IGSM, and hope and pray that each of our students, staff, and faculty members be richly blessed for the sake of His glory at our school.
