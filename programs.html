Our Academic Programs

Doctorate 48 Units

Doctor of Ministry (D.Min)

Program Requirements

Completion of a minimum coursework of 48 units:

  • Tutorial courses: 40 units
  • Dissertation: 8 units

Admission Requirements

The applicant must complete the Master of Ministry program of IGSM with a grade point average of A or above.

Master's 45 Units

Master of Ministry (M.Min)

Program Requirements

Completion of a minimum coursework of 45 units:

  • Required courses: 40 units
  • Elective courses: 8 units
  • Research Project: 6 units

Admission Requirements

  • More than 3 years of missional/pastoral experience with good reputational & ministerial standing.
  • Must complete the DIM Program of IGSM with a grade of B or above.

Required & Elective Curriculum

All students enrolled in any IGSM program are required to complete these core and chosen subjects for graduation.

Required Courses

  • Evangelical Theology
  • Homiletics
  • Covenantal Survey of the Bible
  • Preliminary
  • Discipleship 1 – 3
  • Leadership 1 – 3
  • Mission Perspective
  • Evangelism

Elective Courses

  • Pastoral Counseling
  • Church Planting & Growth
  • Family Ministry
  • Church History
  • Westminster Confession of Faith
  • Prayer Ministry
  • Christian Education
  • Sunday School
  • Pastoral / Missional Ethics

Application Procedure

1. Applicants must submit the following required documents:

  • • IGSM application form
  • • Official transcript from high school or college
  • • Recommendation letters (from a teacher/professor & senior pastor/chairman)
  • • Essay/Testimony detailing personal Christian journey and study motivation (500+ words)
  • • Health certificate indicating physical fitness to pursue studies

2. Admissions Notification

The applicant will be notified directly by the admission office regarding registration instructions.

Scholarship Guidelines

Full Scholarship Offer

All students, excluding those under Bachelor of Ministry and Doctor of Ministry programs, will be offered a full scholarship for their studies at IGSM.

Student Responsibilities

Other miscellaneous expenses—including textbooks and reference materials—are the sole responsibility of the student.
