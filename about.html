Who We Are

About IGSM

Rooted in Scripture, Reaching the World

Our Mission

To Produce Biblical Servant Leaders

IGSM, International Graduate School of Ministry, is to produce servant leaders who are biblically grounded, spiritually mature, and culturally relevant for both the intracultural ministry of the evangelical church in the Philippines and its intercultural ministry abroad.

Spiritual Maturity & Cultural Relevance
Our Vision

To Equip Christian Leaders

IGSM, International Graduate School of Ministry, is here to equip Christian leaders for the kingdom ministry of Christ in world mission through being a school of spiritual and academic excellence.
